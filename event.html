DAY ONE — DECEMBER 10TH

Kickoff

A short introduction from both teams, introducing the startups and their related offerings.

Side-Channel Analysis

~10:00 AM until lunch · led by Smita

  • Brief introduction to symmetric key cryptography
  • Brief introduction to side-channels and faults
  • Lab: SCA and faults on ChipWhisperer Lite

AI-Assisted Secure Chip Design

~3:00 – 5:30 PM · led by Tishya, with Tarun & Hima

  • Introduction to the open-source flow using Yosys
  • Hardware trojans, and why security matters in chip design flows
  • Using AI in EDA: RAG, HyDE, chain-of-thought prompting, and agent loops
  • Lab: SynForce (agentic synthesis) on a 4-bit Galois-field inverter

DAY TWO — DECEMBER 11TH

AI-Assisted Secure Chip Design, continued

~10:00 AM – 2:00 PM

  • Introduction to trojans and equivalence checking
  • Automated verification
  • Lab: NETLAM and VerMI on standard benchmarks
