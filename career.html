PROC SHIELD Private Limited is a deep-tech startup working in hardware security, cryptographic hardware evaluation, and post-quantum security. Our flagship KAVACH platform brings together side-channel and fault-analysis capabilities for cryptographic hardware. Advisor: Prof. Debdeep Mukhopadhyay, IIT Kharagpur.

What you will do

  • Support hardware-security experiments, demonstrations, documentation, and product activities.
  • Assist with basic technical work around side-channel/fault analysis, FPGA/ASIC security, and cryptographic implementations.
  • Maintain and improve the company website and digital presence.
  • Manage social-media communication, technical posts, announcements, and outreach material.
  • Prepare presentations, brochures, project documentation, and coordination material.
  • Support day-to-day startup administration, event/workshop coordination, and follow-up activities.

Who should apply

  • B.Tech or higher in ECE, CSE, or EE.
  • Genuine interest in hardware security and cybersecurity.
  • Comfortable handling both technical and non-technical responsibilities.
  • Good written communication and professional coordination skills.
  • Self-driven, organized, and willing to learn in a startup environment.

Good to have: HTML/CSS/WordPress or similar web tools, Python/C/C++, FPGA/embedded exposure, graphics/content tools, or prior technical outreach experience.

Cross-company exposure: ESP

The role will also support selected technical and non-technical activities for Embedding Security and Privacy (ESP) Pvt Ltd, which works on cryptographic systems, privacy-preserving computation, secure AI/ML, and security training. This may include technical documentation, content/website support, coordination, demos, and project communication.
